# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to keeping the The Online Bakery Platform community respectful, welcoming, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Help maintain a safe and inclusive workspace.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
